about

The Repositional Cipher is a keyboard-based substitution cipher. Q, A, Z, E, D, C always shift to their right neighbor; R, F, V always shift to their left neighbor; all other letters shift left. The cipher is fully reversible with no collisions. Includes optional reverse, mixed-direction, number row, and double-encode modes.
